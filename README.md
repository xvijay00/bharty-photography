- **Minimalist Editorial Black & White Aesthetics**: Clean white canvas inspired by luxury fashion & wedding editorials (Avala style).
- **Official BVV Brand Logo**: Custom circular logo integrated across the navbar, favicon, footer, and booking modal.
- **Split Hero Section**: Narrative text paired with framed real wedding photography slider.

- **Curated Masonry Portfolio**: Categorized tabs (*All, Weddings, Pre-Weddings, Parties & Sangeet, Bridal Portraits*) with high-resolution Lightbox view.
